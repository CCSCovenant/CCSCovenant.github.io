I am an Embodied AI Algorithm Engineer at [Westlake Robotics (西湖机器人)](https://wlrobo.com/). I have been deeply involved in developing [WR1](https://wlrobo.com/wr1), Westlake Robotics' whole-body foundation model for humanoid robots. I received my MPhil in Artificial Intelligence from The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Previously, I graduated from the [Lassonde School of Engineering](https://lassonde.yorku.ca/) at [York University](https://www.yorku.ca/) with a First Class Honours bachelor's degree in Computer Science.
